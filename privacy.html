Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Fidet Personal Integrations is a private application used to provide productivity and personal-assistant functionality to authorized users.

Google User Data

The application may access Google account information and services, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts, when authorized by the user through Google OAuth.

Use of Information

Google user data is used only to perform actions requested by the authorized user, such as reading and organizing email, preparing email drafts, accessing calendar information, and working with files and documents.

Sharing of Information

Google user data is not sold to third parties or used for advertising.

Access and Revocation

Users may revoke the application's access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the developer contact address provided through the application's Google OAuth configuration.
